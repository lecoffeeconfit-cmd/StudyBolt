## Get help with StudyBolt

StudyBolt is operated by Howard Tran, an individual operator in California, United States. For help with sign-in, study packs, subscriptions, or a technical issue, email:

- [lecoffeeconfit@gmail.com](mailto:lecoffeeconfit@gmail.com?subject=StudyBolt%20Support)

Signed-in users can also open **Profile → Help & Support** to send a question and receive a reply in the app. Do not include passwords or confidential course material in a support request.

For an App Store subscription, manage or cancel billing in your Apple account’s Subscriptions settings. Deleting a StudyBolt account does not cancel App Store billing.

Read the [Privacy Policy](https://lecoffeeconfit-cmd.github.io/studybolt-legal/privacypolicy) and [Terms of Service](https://lecoffeeconfit-cmd.github.io/studybolt-legal/termsofservice).
