## Information StudyBolt handles

**Account information.** If you create an account, StudyBolt’s authentication provider receives your email address, password credential, account identifier, and any display name or study profile details you choose to provide. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider; StudyBolt does not store a readable password. You can also use the supported Apple or Google sign-in option when it is configured.

**Study materials and AI requests.** StudyBolt stores your Study Packs and study progress on your device. If you ask StudyBolt to process a file or use a cloud AI feature, the selected file or relevant study text and your submitted question are sent to StudyBolt’s servers and the AI provider needed to return the requested result. The app sends only the current study section and limited neighboring context for StudyCast Tutor requests. The document processor requests non-stored model responses and deletes temporary provider files after processing. Device-based AI, when available, processes the request on your device.

PowerPoint visual-review features may save selected slide-derived images and related visual metadata in private StudyBolt storage so the app can display and analyze those visuals. These are not public unless you separately share content. [Confirm storage retention and cleanup behavior before publication.]

**Sharing and community.** If you choose to share a Study Pack, StudyBolt stores a sanitized copy of its generated notes, flashcards, quiz, and quick-review material. The original uploaded file, source text, quiz history, listening position, and mastery progress are excluded from the share copy. A link-shared pack can be viewed by people who have the link. A public pack can appear in Discover and public class pages. You can revoke a share from the app. Do not share material unless you have the right to do so.

**Support requests.** If you submit a request in Help & Support, StudyBolt stores its category, subject, message, status, and reply with your account so support can respond in the app. Do not include passwords or private course material in a support request.

**Study activity.** The app derives learning insights locally from your study history. When signed in, it may also send limited activity summaries to StudyBolt, such as study event type and time, duration, and aggregate quiz answer/correct counts. These summaries support service operations and account-level usage reporting; they do not include the text of your notes, uploaded source files, or detailed quiz answers.

**Subscriptions and diagnostics.** App Store purchases are processed by Apple and subscription status is synchronized through RevenueCat using your StudyBolt account identifier. StudyBolt does not receive your payment card details. Sentry is used for app diagnostics and may receive crash or technical event information; [confirm the fields and vendor settings for the production build].

## Your choices and account deletion

You can use the Biology sample without uploading a file or creating an account. You can decline notifications and still use the main study tools. You can revoke a share you created. You can request account deletion from **Profile → Account → Delete account**. Account deletion removes the account and associated account-linked records from StudyBolt’s database, and the app clears its local StudyBolt data after the server confirms deletion. A store subscription is billed separately: cancel it in your Apple or Google account before deleting StudyBolt; deleting a StudyBolt account does not cancel store billing.

Study data saved locally can be removed through the app’s account/data reset controls or by removing the app from your device. [Confirm the exact local reset and backup behavior for the shipped version.]

## Retention and security

StudyBolt keeps local Study Packs on your device until you delete or reset them. Account-linked records are kept while needed to provide the account and requested service, and are deleted when you delete your account, subject to limited records that must be retained by law or for security and fraud prevention. Shares remain available until you revoke them, delete the account, or they otherwise expire. Temporary files sent to the AI provider are deleted after processing as described above.
